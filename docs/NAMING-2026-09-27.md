# Naming brainstorm, 2026-09-27 (a Fable subagent's report, for the artist to weigh)

Read-only brainstorm over the project's record; collision checks by web search; PyPI and domains not verified.

## Project name (codename Songbird)

The song grows by itself (music sticks whether you want it or not):
- **Volunteer.** A garden word for a plant that comes up where nobody planted it: the daisy verse, a song arriving unasked, and the people who volunteered to sing. Drawbacks: common word, hard to search, could read as a charity sign-up. No AI-music collision found. Weak as a package name.
- **Unsown.** From the daisy verse. Close to "unsound"; a death-metal album *Unsown and Dead*. Works as a repo or package name.
- **Day's Eye.** The etymology of daisy (opens at dawn, closes at dusk); the song drifts with the date. Few will get it; sounds like "days I". `dayseye` works as a repo name.
- **Bellis.** The daisy's botanical name, echoing Daisy Bell. Sounds like "bellies"; a boutique and a music camp use it.
- **Burr.** From song L. Too cute; reads as irritation.

Passing it hand to hand:
- **Passalong.** Gardeners' term for plants that survive only by being handed on (*Passalong Plants*). Ties the daisy, "hand it along" and CC0. Two small GitHub tools and a defunct 2000s digital-music company used it.
- **Handalong.** From the bridge. Looks like "singalong" misspelt.
- **Hearsay.** Hearing then saying. A UK pop group (2001); sounds legal.
- **Nth Hand.** How each version counts its distance. Sounds like maths; fine as a repo name.

Folk catalogues and song forms:
- **Roundelay.** A short song with a returning refrain. A Newfoundland band, a LAKE album, a GitHub sync server. Slightly olde-worlde.
- **Cantus.** Latin for song, or the melody line. Two well-known choirs (the Norwegian Cantus heard in *Frozen*; the Minneapolis ensemble); PyPI taken.
- **Catch.** An English round; the orange girl "caught it for free"; catchiness. Unsearchable.
- **Trad. Arr.** The folk credit line. *Trad Arr Jones*, the band TRADarrr; `tradarr` looks like a download app. Better as the artist credit ("Trad. arr. your machine").
- **Child 306.** Child's catalogue stops at 305. An in-joke.

## Song title (working title Now It's by You)

From the lyric: **Now It's by You** (the canon couplet always closes, so the line is as fixed as "Nobody knows"; the grammar makes people look twice); **Nobody's Making** (unfinished-sounding); **Water and Wire** (no collision found; sounds old, ends modern; later versions may swap the word); **Yours While You Sing It** (CC0 in plain speech; long, easy to miss); **Still It Grows** (from the grew head; a nod to Still You Grew; a 2003 track "And Still It Grows").

The folk-index idea: **Nobody Knows** with each version subtitled "*first line*, at *n*th hand" (right idea; the title is crowded: Tony Rich, Nik Kershaw, the spiritual); **Nobody Knows (Child 306)** (a wink few will get); **They Saved It as Numbers, at First Hand** (how a version titles itself; for the tags, too long for a list).

Standing apart: **The Passing Song** (could be anyone's); **Day's Eye** (pretty, hides the concept); **Volunteer** (save for the project).

## The subagent's picks

Project: 1 Volunteer, 2 Passalong, 3 Roundelay (Cantus out because of the choirs). Song: 1 Now It's by You, 2 Nobody Knows as the index name inside the program and tags (album Nobody Knows; title *first line*, at *n*th hand; artist Nobody), 3 Water and Wire.

To submit tonight: **Now It's by You, by Volunteer.** Put the folk-index lineage in the metadata and the statement. Check first whether the entry form asks for a team name rather than a project name.

## Decided (2026-09-27, evening)

The artist: the song is **"Now It's by You"** by **"The Volunteers"**, which is also the team name for the
contest entry. The site's address stays songbird.quadule.com, and "Songbird" stays the program's and
repository's name. Every rendition's FLAC is tagged ARTIST "The Volunteers" from here (songbird/cli.py).
