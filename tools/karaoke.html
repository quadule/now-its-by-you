Timing

Takes are recorded in the same clock as the guide, minus the delay your device reports for playback and the mic (not measured yet). Browsers under-report it, Bluetooth headphones most of all, so every take is also synced automatically against the song, and what that finds is remembered here as the extra correction. To check it yourself, calibrate: press the button and tap, clap or say "ta" on each of the clicks.
