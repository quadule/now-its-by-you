# C. Somebody Sang It to Me

*Lullaby. Rocking 3/4 with a long-short-medium lilt, plain major. Lines of ten and seven syllables, ABCB.*

> Nobody wrote it and nobody owns it;
> somebody sang it to me.
> I'll sing it to you, and you'll sing it to someone,
> someone I never will see.

> I could have kept it alone in my head,
> safe as a stone in a well;
> down it would go when I went to my rest,
> nobody left who could tell.

> I could have written it down on a page,
> folded and put on a shelf;
> paper will yellow and language will change
> till it is strange to itself.

> I could have saved it as numbers and light,
> copied as quick as a wink;
> bright as it is, when the power goes out,
> darker than water or ink.

> I could have shown it to all of the world,
> lit up in everyone's hand;
> everyone watches and nobody sings,
> gone like a print in the sand.

> So I just sang it the way that it came,
> hummed it without all the words;
> songs need no paper or power or name,
> only a mouth, like the birds.
