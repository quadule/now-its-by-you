# A. Nobody Knows

*Narrative ballad. Slow 3/4, dorian. Four-line verses in anapestic tetrameter, AABB.*

The idea passes through five keepers, one per verse, and the last one sings it
in a language the first one never spoke.

> Who made this song? Nobody knows.
> It came through the window and out it goes.
> It's yours while you sing it, and then it's gone,
> so learn it by heart, and then pass it on.

> There once was a thought in a quiet man's head;
> he kept it so close that it never was said.
> It walked where he walked, and it slept where he slept;
> they buried it with him, the secret he kept.

> A girl wrote it down by a candle one night,
> in ink on a page, and she folded it tight.
> It sailed the gray water and slept in a drawer,
> and nobody speaks like she wrote anymore.

> They saved it as numbers, a flicker of light,
> and copied it round the whole world in a night.
> Each copy was perfect in every last part,
> but not one machine ever knew it by heart.

> The whole world was watching it all in a day,
> a million bright faces all lit the same way.
> They laughed and they wept, and they scrolled on and on,
> and nobody sang it, and then it was gone.

> A sailor was humming it down by the quay;
> a girl selling oranges caught it for free.
> She hadn't his language, she hadn't his name;
> she gave it her own words and sang it the same.
