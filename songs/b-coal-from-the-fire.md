# B. Coal from the Fire

*Riddle song in the shape of "Who Killed Cock Robin". Lilting 6/8, mixolydian.
Each verse: four short question-and-answer lines, then a couplet telling what happened.*

> Carry it over the water and wire,
> hand it along like a coal from the fire;
> it's nobody's making and everyone's own,
> it dies in the dark if you leave it alone.

> Who'll keep it safe? / "I," said the Mind, / "close and confined, / I'll keep it safe."
> It slept in his head till the day that he died;
> they buried the Mind with the thought still inside.

> Who'll write it down? / "I," said the Ink, / "dry in a blink, / I'll write it down."
> It lay in a drawer for a hundred-odd years;
> it speaks in a voice that now nobody hears.

> Who'll send it far? / "I," said the Wire, / "quicker than fire, / I'll send it far."
> It flew round the world in the wink of an eye;
> it lived in a box till they let the box die.

> Who'll show the world? / "I," said the Screen, / "brightest you've seen, / I'll show the world."
> A billion eyes watched it and scrolled right along,
> but nobody hummed it; it wasn't a song.

> Who made this song? / "No," said the Bird, / "sang what I heard; / it's not my song."
> The Bird had it off of a girl in the lane,
> who'd had it from someone who'd had it the same.

> Who made this song? / "No," said the Code, / "only the road; / it's not my song."
> A thousand have made it, and none of them knew;
> by everyone, no one, and now it's by you.
