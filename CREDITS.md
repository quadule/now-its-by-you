# Credits

**Now It's by You** and Songbird are by Milo Winningham.

## Singers

The first version of the song (September 2026) was shaped by eight people who sang along to it online. The
numbers taken from how they sang (timing, length, loudness, how the pitch moves into and out of each note) are
blended into the song's machine singer, and its voice learned the words from them. Their recordings were
deleted by September 30, 2026, as promised; no one's voice travels with the song.
