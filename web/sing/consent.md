**Singing for the first version of the song closes on Sunday, September 27, 2026 at 10 PM Pacific time** (05:00 UTC
on Monday, September 28). You can still sing after that: what you send then may go into a later version rather
than the first.

## What happens to what you sing

- **What the song keeps from you is how you sing, not what you sound like.** From your takes the
  program takes a few numbers for each note: how early or late you come in, how long you hold it, how
  loud it is, how the pitch moves into and out of it. Your voice can't be rebuilt from them. Blended
  with everyone else's, they gradually change how the song's machine singer sings in every version of
  the song made after you (you can hear that singer here, in the whole-song guide).
- **The machine's voice may also learn the words from you.** Once, after singing closes and before
  September 30, the recordings may be used to help the song's voice say its words more clearly. It's
  trained on everyone's takes at once, and it isn't set to sound like any one person.
- **Your recordings stay private, and they're deleted.** They're never published or shared, and they
  never travel with the song. They wait in private storage and on the artist's computer, and are
  **deleted by the end of September 30, 2026** (Pacific time), sooner if you delete them yourself.
- **Your own page.** When you start, you get a private link (on screen, and by email) where you can
  listen to what you sent and delete it. Your version of the song (your voice in the sections you sang)
  and your vocals alone will appear there to download before September 30, and are deleted with the
  rest. Anyone with the link can use it, so keep it to yourself.
- **You can change your mind until singing closes.** Until 10 PM Pacific on Sunday, September 27 you
  can withdraw from your page, and your recordings and the numbers taken from them are deleted. Withdrawal
  closes when singing does: that night the song is finished from everyone's singing and entered in a
  competition, so after that what you gave stays part of it. You can still delete your recordings until
  September 30, but not the numbers already taken from them.
- **What travels with the song** is only the numbers and the voice, blended from everyone, free for
  anyone to use (CC0, public domain), and your name if you'd like to be credited.
- **Your email** is used only to send you your page and your version, and is deleted by the end of
  September 30, unless you ask to hear when the song is released to the public: then it's kept for that one message.
- **Who's asking:** Songbird is by Milo Winningham. Questions, or anything else: write to
  milo@winningham.net.
