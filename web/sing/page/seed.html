Now It's by You

Now It's by You is a folk song passed between people and computers that explores music's ability to take on a life of its own, in verse and by embedding the software that sang it.

Run the program and you get a new version of the song, shaped by your computer and the season. Sing into it and it learns a little of your timing and inflection (your recording is deleted right after, and your voice is never cloned). The piece confronts the blurred authorship of AI music through the lens of folk music and oral tradition: it wonders who made it, and invites you to take part.

Who made it

The music you hear is made by a small computer program, and every version it sings is a little different. The program and its song were written with commercial generative AI models as collaborators. The finished program is not dependent on any online service; it runs on an average computer using traditional software synthesis methods for instrumentation and a small offline model for the voice.

Eight people sang into the first version. {{CREDITS}}
