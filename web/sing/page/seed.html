Now It's by You

A folk song passed between people and computers. Who made this song? Nobody knows.

The file below is the song, and it is also the program that sang it. Play it and you hear this version. Run it and it sings a new one, on your computer and your day, that carries a little of everyone who sang into it before you.

With Python 3.11 or newer installed, in a terminal:

python3 {{SEED_FILE}}          what this is, and what you can do
python3 {{SEED_FILE}} new      sing a new version here, today
python3 {{SEED_FILE}} studio   change it, sing into it, record your own

On Windows, py instead of python3. The first run fetches its libraries (about 100 MB) into a folder of its own and installs nothing else. It says what it will do and asks before it writes anything.

About the AI in this

The music you hear is made by a small computer program running on the artist's home computer, and every version it sings is a little different. The program and its song were written with commercial generative AI models as collaborators. The finished program is not dependent on any online service; it runs on an average computer using traditional software synthesis methods for instrumentation and a small offline model for the voice.

The song, its program and every version are in the public domain: they belong to no one. The people who sang into it lent their timing and inflection as a handful of numbers; their recordings are deleted, and no voice is cloned. Singing into the shared version is by invitation.
