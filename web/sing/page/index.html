1 What this is

This is a folk song that passes itself on: every copy carries the small program that sings it, and each time it runs, it sings the song again a little differently. Its singer is synthetic, and it learns how people sing (where you breathe, lean on a word, slide into a note) from recordings like the ones you're about to make.

Put headphones on, sing along with the guide in your own way, and send the takes you like. Wrong notes are fine.

4 Microphone and timing

Headphones on, so the guide stays out of the microphone. Then let the page hear you: sing or say something and watch the level move.

Every phone and pair of headphones delays the sound a little. To measure yours, press the button and tap the microphone (or clap, or say "ta") on each click after the first four. It takes ten seconds and helps your takes line up.

5 What you'll hear

The tune alone leaves the singing to you. You can switch between takes.
