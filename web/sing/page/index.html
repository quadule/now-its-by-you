3 Microphone and timing

Headphones on, so the guide stays out of the microphone. Wired ones are best: Bluetooth headphones drop to telephone quality when the microphone opens. Then let the page hear you: sing or say something and watch the level move.

Every phone and pair of headphones delays the sound a little. To measure yours, press the button and tap the microphone (or clap, or say "ta") on each click after the first four. It takes ten seconds and helps your takes line up.
